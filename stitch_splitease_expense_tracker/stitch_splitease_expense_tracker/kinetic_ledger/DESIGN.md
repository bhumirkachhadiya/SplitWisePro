## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, high-precision fintech aesthetic designed for collaborative finance, shared ledgers, and group bill settling. Bridging the utilitarian clarity of Linear with the kinetic luxury of Revolut and the approachability of Splitwise, the system elevates social transactions from awkward chore to an effortless, frictionless ritual.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Clarity Over Clutter:** Dense financial information is partitioned through structural whitespace, hairline boundaries, and tonal hierarchy rather than noisy containers.
- **Mathematical Tactility:** Crisp card structures, calibrated dual-shadow elevations, and purposeful micro-interactions evoke the confidence of premium engineering.
- **High-Affordance Ledger Mechanics:** Status states—specifically balances owed, balances collected, and settled statuses—utilize unmistakable semantic colorways paired with unambiguous typographic treatments.
- **Modern Minimalist Utility:** Surfaces default to ultra-clean canvases with sharp contrast ratios, avoiding muddy gradients in favor of sharp accents and luminous interactive states.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-energy emeralds anchored by architectural navies and cool slates to convey stability, trust, and speed.

### Primary & Accent Roles
- **Primary Emerald (`#16A878`):** The primary engine of the system. Drives key actions, primary interactive controls, settled ledger confirmations, and high-priority navigation points.
- **Secondary Forest Accent (`#087F5B`):** Deep emerald used for high-contrast active states, hover transitions on primary actions, and prominent brand accents.
- **Tertiary Deep Navy (`#172B3A`):** The foundational ink. Applied across all primary headings, key figures, and dark interactive elements to eliminate the harshness of pure black while retaining maximum legibility.

### Surface & Foundation Tokens
- **Canvas / App Background (`#F8FAFC`):** Cool, crisp slate-tinted white that grounds the interface and provides distinct separation for raised cards.
- **Surface / Card Background (`#FFFFFF`):** High-purity white for elevated cards, modal drawers, and tabular data rows.
- **Subtle Surface (`#F1F5F9`):** Subdued fill for nested table headers, secondary buttons, and balance chip containers.
- **Border / Structural Divider (`#E2E8F0`):** Soft, structural boundary used across borders, cards, and input fields.
- **Border Subtle (`#F1F5F9`):** Low-contrast dividers inside multi-item ledger feeds.

### Semantic Tokens
- **Positive / You Are Owed (`#22C55E`):** Bright emerald indicator signifying credit, positive ledger drift, and successful settlement.
- **Negative / You Owe (`#EF4444`):** Crisp, vivid red applied strictly to debts, destructive actions, and overdue obligations.
- **Attention / Pending (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber used for unresolved balance requests, reminders, and split discrepancies.
- **Secondary Neutral Text (`#718096`):** Balanced slate for metadata, timestamp logs, secondary labels, and helper copy.

## Typography

The typographic engine is built on **Inter**, optimized for screen legibility, dense tabular views, and high-frequency scan rates.

### Tabular Numerics
All balance readouts, currency expressions (₹ INR), split percentages, and transaction lists strictly enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1, "cv11" 1` (tabular figures and curated alternate glyphs). This prevents layout shifts during live data re-renders and aligns decimal places across financial lists.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings deliver an editorial, product-first tone.
- **Numbers as Signposts:** Dedicated `numeric-*` tokens ensure balance summaries stand distinct from descriptive item names.
- **Micro-Labels:** `label-sm` utilizes uppercase formatting and subtle positive tracking (`0.04em`) exclusively for status identifiers, category tags, and ledger dates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a responsive 12-column fluid grid system paired with strict 8pt base increments (with 4pt micro-steps).

### Responsive Grid Model
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max content boundary `1240px`, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Three-column master layout: Global Nav (240px fixed), Ledger Feed / Activity Stream (fluid 7 cols), Real-time Group Breakdown / Summary Widget (fluid 5 cols).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Two-column adaptive layout: Collapsible rail menu, stacked activity and balance drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`. Single-column stack with persistent floating bottom action bar ("+ Add Expense" / "Settle Up").

### Spatial Density
- Internal card padding defaults to `space-xl` (24px) on desktop and scales down to `space-lg` (16px) on mobile viewports.
- Line items inside expense streams use `space-md` (12px) vertical padding to maintain balanced scannability without requiring excessive vertical scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined multi-layered ambient drop shadow system combined with crisp, low-contrast border boundaries. Interfaces remain bright and airy while providing deep physical presence.

### Layer Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (Elevation 0):** Flat background surface (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow. Holds structural dividing lines.
2. **Card Base (Elevation 1):** Primary content containers, expense cards, and user balance cards.
   - Dual-shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.03)`
   - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
3. **Interactive & Hover (Elevation 2):** Hovered feed items, active transaction cards, dropdown menus, and popovers.
   - Dual-shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.04)`
   - Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`
4. **Overlay / Drawer / Modal (Elevation 3):** Quick-split modals, bottom settlement drawers, and command menus.
   - Dual-shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.04)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`
   - Backdrop: `rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric contour model, leveraging medium-to-large radiuses (12px–16px) across cards and modal interfaces to balance technical precision with approachable modern fintech design.

### Corner Radius Allocation
- **Cards & Modals (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 16px):** Primary content cards, group dashboards, balance cards, and settlement sheets.
- **Controls & Inputs (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 12px):** Form input fields, buttons, split pill selectors, and user avatar badges.
- **Chips & Badges (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Financial balance chips, debt tags, and status pills.
- **Nested Inner Surfaces (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Inner nested stat blocks, icon badges, and calculation steps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#16A878` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `border-radius: 12px`, padding `10px 20px`. Subtle scale down (`0.98`) on active tap. Hover transition to `#087F5B`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `#172B3A` text. Hover background shifts to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** `#FEE2E2` background with `#EF4444` text. Hover shifts to `#EF4444` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Balance Chips & Status Indicators
- **Positive Balance ("You are owed"):** Container fill `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`. Displays tabular numeric values prefixed with `+ ₹`.
- **Negative Balance ("You owe"):** Container fill `#FEE2E2`, text `#B91C1C`, border `1px solid #FECACA`. Displays tabular numeric values prefixed with `- ₹`.
- **Settled / Neutral:** Container fill `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Displays `Settled Up`.

### Expense Lists & Feed Rows
- Single item rows are wrapped in cards with `border-radius: 12px`, `background: #FFFFFF`, and subtle hover elevation.
- **Left Slot:** Category icon badge (`40x40px`, `border-radius: 10px`, contextual pastel tinted background, crisp monochrome icon).
- **Center Slot:** Expense title (`title-md`), followed by date and payer details (`body-sm`, `#718096`).
- **Right Slot:** Individual share status on top (`body-sm`), followed by the aggregate total (`numeric-md`, tabular figures).

### Input Fields & Split Calculators
- Default state: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `12px 16px`, placeholder color `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border color `#16A878`, box shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 168, 120, 0.15)`.
- Currency Input: Features a fixed, non-selectable ₹ symbol rendered in deep navy (`#172B3A`), with dynamic type scaling for large balance entry.

### Cards
- **Ledger Card:** Top summary container with a 2-column or 3-column split showing total group spend, your net balance, and immediate action buttons ("Add Expense", "Settle Up").
- High-contrast separation with 1px border lines and 16px corner radius.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes, Radio, & Split Pills)
- Checkboxes and radio buttons feature a solid `#16A878` fill with a pure white check/dot when selected. Inactive border is `#CBD5E1`.
- Split distribution selector buttons (Equal, Exact, Percentage, Shares) use an enclosed segmented control with `#F1F5F9` track and elevated white active indicator tabs.
